# Scoring rubric

Score every company 1-5 on each factor (half points allowed). Score only on facts you found, and score unknowns 2 with a note rather than guessing high.

| Key | Factor | Default weight | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D | Founder domain insight | 20% | No prior exposure to the problem | A few years adjacent to the problem | 10+ years inside the problem, or ran the function at scale |
| X | Founder prior exits / scaling | 8% | First job or first company | Early employee or exec at a venture-backed company | Founded and sold a company, or senior exec through a large exit |
| T | Technical and product depth | 7% | No technical co-founder; product outsourced | Competent product and engineering lead | PhD, research faculty or principal engineer; AI in production |
| C | Team completeness | 10% | Solo founder with gaps in key roles | Two founders with one clear gap | Complementary CEO, CTO and go-to-market, all full-time |
| M | Market size (bottom-up) | 20% | Under $100M | $300M-$1B | Over $1B with clear adjacent expansion |
| TR | Traction | 12% | Pre-product or no customers named | Paying pilots or named customers | Marquee customers plus growth or retention data |
| F | Fit with this fund | 15% | Outside its geography or past its stage | In region but awkward: services-heavy, capital-heavy, already raised a lot, cap-table conflict | In region, in its sectors, and the fund can be 1st or 2nd institutional money at its check size |
| TM | Timing | 8% | Just raised a large round | Next raise likely in 12-24 months | Raising now or within 6 months and needs a lead |

Founder factors (D, X, T, C) total 45%.

## Why these defaults

The defaults come from backtesting one early-stage fund's first checks against their outcomes. Domain insight and market size separated winners from losers best. Team completeness and fit helped. Prior exits and traction helped only weakly: famous repeat founders and early traction both appeared among failures. Timing did not predict outcomes at all; it stays in the rubric because it tells the fund whom to call this quarter. Step 5 of the workflow re-tunes these weights on the target fund's own history.

## Practical notes

- **F is fund-specific.** A strong company the fund cannot lead (too late, too expensive, out of region) scores low on F even if it scores high everywhere else.
- **TM measures months since the last round.** Seed rounds typically come 12-24 months apart, so a round that closed last month scores 1-2.
- **Company-reported traction** can still score well, but say in the note that the figure comes from the company.
- **Hired CEOs.** If the CEO joined after founding, score D and X on the CEO but note that the original founders are unknown, and cap C at 3.5.
